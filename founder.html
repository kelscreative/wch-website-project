Founder and Leadership

Wisdom and Counsel House is stewarded by Founder and Lead Minister Harrison Ihunwa, operating under spiritual oversight, biblical testing, and committed structural accountability.

Founder & Lead Minister

Harrison Ihunwa

Harrison Ihunwa is the Founder and Lead Minister of Wisdom and Counsel House. The Founder carries responsibility for WCH’s vision, doctrine, direction and development, while remaining accountable to God, Scripture and spiritual leadership. No revelation places the Founder above responsible Christian conduct and biblical standards of integrity.

"Our primary longing is not merely to speak into human destinies, but to align the inward man with the holiness and sovereign wisdom of Almighty God."
— Harrison Ihunwa

Leadership and Governance Structure

The leadership and governance structure of Wisdom and Counsel House comprises:

(1) Spiritual Oversight: Through House of Worship Christian Church (HOWC), Hammersmith, West London, led by Pastors E.J. and Sarah Lavapie.
(2) Founder and Lead Minister: Harrison Ihunwa, carrying responsibility for vision, doctrine, direction and development.
(3) WCH Leadership: Supporting vision, healthy governance, safeguarding and development.
(4) Chamber Leads: Leading operational execution across the Five Chambers of Ministry.
(5) Ministry Teams: Partners, volunteers, worship ministers and workers appointed according to character, competence, maturity, gifting and ministry needs.

Christian Experience & Ministry Journey

The Founder’s personal journey encompasses a profound testimony of God's preserving power through survival of a life-threatening road accident, followed by academic studies and graduation from Central University College.

Grounded in personal gratitude, consecration, and Christian discipline, this experience informs WCH’s solemn emphasis on spiritual sobriety, character formation, humility, and unwavering fidelity to Christ and His Church.

Accountability Commitments

Accountability commitments include responsible financial handling, testing prophetic ministry, protecting vulnerable people, supporting ministry workers and ensuring no individual becomes indispensable to the work.

Meeting Locations

Wisdom and Counsel House (WCH) currently operates in the London Borough of Wandsworth, United Kingdom. Our community ministry gatherings are intended to take place in hired community halls, club rooms, and other suitable meeting spaces.

Our choice venues include Focus Hall and Newlands Hall, subject to availability and hire arrangements.

Hiring/Availability Venue Reference:
Focus Hall, Newlands Hall, Wandsworth, London SW18/SW19

Important notice: WCH does not currently have a permanent dedicated ministry building. All meeting locations are community/partner gatherings. Focus Hall and Newlands Hall are external hire venues, not permanent owned properties. Venues receive community rentals and leases, subject to booking and confirmation.
