The Four Pillars of the House

Every teaching, discourse, and prophetic gathering rests upon four unchanging biblical stanchions. They move believers from divine hearing into practical Christian responsibility and enduring spiritual formation.

01 — Wisdom

Understanding and applying God’s ways in life, ministry, relationships, decisions, character, stewardship, communication, work/business, family and service.

Scriptural Focus: "Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding." — Proverbs 4:7

02 — Revelation

Learning to recognise, receive, test, understand and responsibly steward what God communicates. Teaching may include hearing God, Scripture and how God communicates, prophetic ministry, discernment, interpretation, obedience and prophetic responsibility. Revelation must lead to obedience and responsibility, not be pursued for its own sake.

Scriptural Focus: "Prove all things; hold fast that which is good." — 1 Thessalonians 5:21

03 — Counsel

Helping people navigate life with biblical wisdom and godly guidance. Areas may include relationships, decisions, personal development, character formation, conflict resolution, reconciliation and practical life matters. Counsel does not replace professional, medical, legal or other specialist services where required.

Scriptural Focus: "Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety." — Proverbs 11:14

04 — Formation

Moving people beyond information into maturity, Christlike character, humility, discipline, obedience, accountability, wisdom, service, leadership and responsibility.

Scriptural Focus: "My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you." — Galatians 4:19

Meeting Locations

Wisdom and Counsel House (WCH) currently operates in the London Borough of Wandsworth, United Kingdom. Our community ministry gatherings are intended to take place in hired community halls, club rooms, and other suitable meeting spaces.

Our choice venues include Focus Hall and Newlands Hall, subject to availability and hire arrangements.

Hiring/Availability Venue Reference:
Focus Hall, Newlands Hall, Wandsworth, London SW18/SW19

Important notice: WCH does not currently have a permanent dedicated ministry building. All meeting locations are community/partner gatherings. Focus Hall and Newlands Hall are external hire venues, not permanent owned properties. Venues receive community rentals and leases, subject to booking and confirmation.
