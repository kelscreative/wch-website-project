2. About WCH
Wisdom and Counsel House began in the United Kingdom in 2022 through early digital expressions, including Clubhouse, and interpersonal ministry. It has developed into a wider ministry house and movement expressed through people, prayer, teaching, prophetic ministry, discipleship, fellowship, media, missions and publishing.
WCH is prophetic and evangelistic. It may gather in churches, halls, homes, conferences, nations, communities, universities and digital spaces. Wherever it gathers, it represents the House; its location may change, but its calling remains the same.
WCH is not a church and does not intend to become one. It exists alongside local churches and serves the wider Body of Christ. It is a distinct ministry with its own vision, calling, leadership and expression.
Mandate: to teach people how to hear and respond to God; provide godly counsel; form people in character, responsibility and service; reveal the mind of God; create environments for prayer and worship; produce books, teaching materials and Christian media; and strengthen the wider Body of Christ.
Core values from the latest blueprint: Christ, Scripture, Wisdom, Truth, Prayer, Obedience, Character, Accountability, Service, Excellence, Community and Multiplication.
Ministry culture: Biblical but not rigid; prophetic but not reckless; spiritual but not strange; wise but not complicated; deep but accessible; structured but not institutionalised; excellent but not performative; relational but not dependent; powerful but accountable.
Statement of faith
WCH believes in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit; that the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the authoritative foundation for Christian faith and life; that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, who died for our sins, was buried and rose bodily on the third day; and that salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. WCH believes the Holy Spirit indwells, empowers and guides believers; the Church is the Body of Christ; God still speaks and leads His people, with all revelation tested against Scripture; the gifts of the Holy Spirit remain relevant to the Church; Christian maturity requires faith, obedience, holiness, wisdom, love and service; and Jesus Christ will return, and God will ultimately establish His purposes. Amen and Amen.
This wording follows the statement of faith in the blueprint. The Founder and Lead Minister should approve the final presentation before publication.
Spiritual oversight and accountability
The blueprint identifies House of Worship Christian Church (HOWC), Hammersmith, West London, as WCH’s home church and names Pastors E.J. and Sarah Lavapie as its spiritual and pastoral oversight. The relationship provides pastoral counsel, doctrinal oversight, ministry guidance and accountability, but does not make WCH a department of HOWC. Obtain approval from the Founder and relevant HOWC leadership for this public wording before publication.
3. The Four Pillars
Wisdom
Understanding and applying God’s ways in life, ministry, relationships, decisions, character, stewardship, communication, work/business, family and service.
Revelation
Learning to recognise, receive, test, understand and responsibly steward what God communicates. Teaching may include hearing God, Scripture and how God communicates, prophetic ministry, discernment, interpretation, obedience and prophetic responsibility. Revelation must lead to obedience and responsibility, not be pursued for its own sake.
Counsel
Helping people navigate life with biblical wisdom and godly guidance. Areas may include relationships, decisions, personal development, character formation, conflict resolution, reconciliation and practical life matters. Counsel does not replace professional, medical, legal or other specialist services where required.
Formation
Moving people beyond information into maturity, Christlike character, humility, discipline, obedience, accountability, wisdom, service, leadership and responsibility.
Use existing, approved teachings and resources where available. Do not invent course titles or claim a programme is running unless it has been confirmed.
4. The Five Chambers
01 — House of Prayer
The prayer expression of WCH, devoted to seeking God through intercession, worship and consecration. Expressions may include prophetic entreaties, prayer gatherings, intercession sessions, worship, fasting, consecration and revival meetings.
02 — School of Wisdom & Counsel (SWC)
The teaching expression of WCH. It equips people through Bible teaching, courses, seminars, workshops, ministry training, leadership development, wisdom teaching, prophetic instruction and practical life teaching. It is not presented as a formal theological institution.
03 — Company of Prophets (COP)
A relationship-based environment for people called to prophetic ministry or developing in the prophetic. Its purpose includes prophetic training and practice, accountability, study of the Scriptural prophets, fellowship and edification.
04 — Digital House
The media and digital expression of WCH, which may include livestreams, online teaching, audio teachings, podcasts, social media, digital courses, online prayer and digital resources.
05 — Publishing House
Important status note: The blueprint defines the five chambers and their intended functions, but does not confirm the current meeting schedules, operational status of each programme or individual chamber leaders. Present these as ministry expressions and intended functions unless WCH confirms that a specific activity is currently running.

Leadership and governance
The blueprint describes the following structure: (1) spiritual oversight through HOWC; (2) Founder and Lead Minister; (3) WCH leadership supporting vision, healthy governance, safeguarding and development; (4) chamber leads where appropriate; and (5) ministry teams, partners, volunteers, worship ministers and workers appointed according to character, competence, maturity, gifting and ministry needs.
Accountability commitments include responsible financial handling, testing prophetic ministry, protecting vulnerable people, supporting ministry workers and ensuring no individual becomes indispensable to the work.
