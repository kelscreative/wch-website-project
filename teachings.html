Teachings and Resources

Wisdom and Counsel House provides books, teaching manuals, devotionals, study materials, articles, audio teachings, and podcasts to edify and equip believers.

Publications & Teaching Materials

Wisdom and Counsel House publishes and distributes books, foundational study manuals, devotionals, and audio discourses designed to facilitate personal formation and sound biblical understanding.

Works in Development

The founder materials refer to works including Hearing God, The Art of Understanding People and a title rendered in the supplied materials as both “More Than a Rushing Mighty Wind” and “More Than a Mighty Rushing Wind”.

MINDCAST Media Expression

MINDCAST serves as a related media expression of Harrison Ihunwa Ministries (HIM) in alignment with Wisdom and Counsel House, delivering deep reflective conversations and apostolic insights.

Meeting Locations

Wisdom and Counsel House (WCH) currently operates in the London Borough of Wandsworth, United Kingdom. Our community ministry gatherings are intended to take place in hired community halls, club rooms, and other suitable meeting spaces.

Our choice venues include Focus Hall and Newlands Hall, subject to availability and hire arrangements.

Location:
Focus Hall, Newlands Hall, Wandsworth, London SW18/SW19

Important notice: WCH does not currently have a permanent dedicated ministry building. All meeting locations are community/partner gatherings. Focus Hall and Newlands Hall are external hire venues, not permanent owned properties. Venues receive community rentals and leases, subject to booking and confirmation.
