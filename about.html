Ministry Inquiry

About Wisdom and Counsel House

Wisdom and Counsel House began in the United Kingdom in 2022 through early digital expressions, including Clubhouse, and interpersonal ministry. It has developed into a wider ministry house and movement expressed through people, prayer, teaching, prophetic ministry, discipleship, fellowship, media, missions and publishing.

Nature of the Ministry

WCH is prophetic and evangelistic. It may gather in churches, halls, homes, conferences, nations, communities, universities and digital spaces. Wherever it gathers, it represents the House; its location may change, but its calling remains the same.

A Ministry House, Not a Local Church

WCH is not a church and does not intend to become one. It exists alongside local churches and serves the wider Body of Christ. It is a distinct ministry with its own vision, calling, leadership and expression.

Our Mandate

To teach people how to hear and respond to God; provide godly counsel; form people in character, responsibility and service; reveal the mind of God; create environments for prayer and worship; produce books, teaching materials and Christian media; and strengthen the wider Body of Christ.

Core Values

The life, teaching, fellowship, and operations of Wisdom and Counsel House are governed by twelve biblical stanchions:

Christ Scripture Wisdom Truth Prayer Obedience Character Accountability Service Excellence Community Multiplication

Ministry Culture

The practical disposition of the House balances spiritual authority with sobriety and humility:

Biblical but not rigid
Prophetic but not reckless
Spiritual but not strange
Wise but not complicated
Deep but accessible
Structured but not institutionalised
Excellent but not performative
Relational but not dependent
Powerful but accountable

Statement of Faith

"WCH believes in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit; that the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the authoritative foundation for Christian faith and life; that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, who died for our sins, was buried and rose bodily on the third day; and that salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. WCH believes the Holy Spirit indwells, empowers and guides believers; the Church is the Body of Christ; God still speaks and leads His people, with all revelation tested against Scripture; the gifts of the Holy Spirit remain relevant to the Church; Christian maturity requires faith, obedience, holiness, wisdom, love and service; and Jesus Christ will return, and God will ultimately establish His purposes. Amen and Amen."

Spiritual Oversight and Accountability

Wisdom and Counsel House recognizes House of Worship Christian Church (HOWC), Hammersmith, West London, as its home church under Pastors E.J. and Sarah Lavapie for spiritual and pastoral oversight. The relationship provides pastoral counsel, doctrinal oversight, ministry guidance and accountability, while maintaining WCH’s distinct apostolic calling.

Meeting Locations

Wisdom and Counsel House (WCH) currently operates in the London Borough of Wandsworth, United Kingdom. Our community ministry gatherings are intended to take place in hired community halls, club rooms, and other suitable meeting spaces.

Our choice venues include Focus Hall and Newlands Hall, subject to availability and hire arrangements.

Hiring/Availability Venue Reference:
Focus Hall, Newlands Hall, Wandsworth, London SW18/SW19

Important notice: WCH does not currently have a permanent dedicated ministry building. All meeting locations are community/partner gatherings. Focus Hall and Newlands Hall are external hire venues, not permanent owned properties. Venues receive community rentals and leases, subject to booking and confirmation.
