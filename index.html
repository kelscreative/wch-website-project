The Four Pillars of the House

Every teaching, discourse, and prophetic gathering rests upon four unchanging biblical stanchions.

Pillar 01

Wisdom

Understanding and applying God’s ways in life, ministry, relationships, decisions, character, stewardship, communication, work/business, family and service.

Proverbs 4 | Ecclesiastes 10
Pillar 02

Revelation

Learning to recognise, receive, test, understand and responsibly steward what God communicates. Revelation must lead to obedience and responsibility, not be pursued for its own sake.

1 Thess 5:21 | 2 Tim 3:16
Pillar 03

Counsel

Helping people navigate life with biblical wisdom and godly guidance. Areas include relationships, decisions, personal development, character formation, conflict resolution, reconciliation and practical life matters.

Proverbs 11:14 | Isaiah 11:2
Pillar 04

Formation

Moving people beyond information into maturity, Christlike character, humility, discipline, obedience, accountability, wisdom, service, leadership and responsibility.

Galatians 4:19 | Ephesians 4:13

About Wisdom and Counsel House

Origins & Distinct Identity

Wisdom and Counsel House began in the United Kingdom in 2022 through early digital expressions, including Clubhouse, and interpersonal ministry. It has developed into a wider ministry house and movement expressed through people, prayer, teaching, prophetic ministry, discipleship, fellowship, media, missions and publishing.

WCH is not a church and does not intend to become one. It exists alongside local churches and serves the wider Body of Christ. It is a distinct ministry with its own vision, calling, leadership and expression.

Mandate & Calling

To teach people how to hear and respond to God; provide godly counsel; form people in character, responsibility and service; reveal the mind of God; create environments for prayer and worship; produce books, teaching materials and Christian media; and strengthen the wider Body of Christ.

Ministry Culture

Biblical but not rigid; prophetic but not reckless; spiritual but not strange; wise but not complicated; deep but accessible; structured but not institutionalised; excellent but not performative; relational but not dependent; powerful but accountable.

Statement of Faith

WCH believes in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit; that the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the authoritative foundation for Christian faith and life; that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, who died for our sins, was buried and rose bodily on the third day; and that salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

WCH believes the Holy Spirit indwells, empowers and guides believers; the Church is the Body of Christ; God still speaks and leads His people, with all revelation tested against Scripture; the gifts of the Holy Spirit remain relevant to the Church; Christian maturity requires faith, obedience, holiness, wisdom, love and service; and Jesus Christ will return, and God will ultimately establish His purposes. Amen.

Spiritual Oversight & Accountability

Wisdom and Counsel House recognizes House of Worship Christian Church (HOWC), Hammersmith, West London, as its home church under Pastors E.J. and Sarah Lavapie for spiritual and pastoral oversight. The relationship provides pastoral counsel, doctrinal oversight, ministry guidance and accountability.

Ministry Principles & Prophetic Ethics

Prophetic Ethics & Conduct

Public content and ministry practices reflect WCH's foundational commitments:

  • Honour Scripture and the Lord Jesus Christ as supreme.
  • Respect human responsibility and moral agency.
  • Avoid manipulation, intimidation, financial exploitation, unnecessary exposure of private information, and unhealthy psychological dependency.
  • Welcome biblical testing, theological scrutiny, and spiritual accountability.
  • Recognise the possibility of human error and distinguish genuine prophecy from personal opinion.
  • Encourage obedience to God rather than uncritical allegiance to the minister.

Ministry Scope & Spiritual Counsel Disclaimer

Biblical and pastoral counsel provided by WCH is spiritual in nature and grounded in Christian Scripture. It does not replace professional, medical, psychiatric, legal, financial, or other specialist licensed services where required.

Privacy & Data Respect

Wisdom and Counsel House handles all inquiries with strict discretion and care. Contact information submitted is used solely for responding to legitimate ministry correspondence and is never sold, commodified, or shared with third parties.

Safeguarding Ethics & Pastoral Care

Commitment to Protection & Welfare

Wisdom and Counsel House is dedicated to maintaining safe, respectful, and transparent environments for all gatherings, whether physical or digital.

Our leadership and ministry workers are committed to protecting vulnerable individuals, exercising pastoral vigilance, and ensuring that no ministry practice causes harm, distress, or exploitation.

Leadership Accountability

In accordance with ministry governance, the Founder and Lead Minister remains accountable to God, Scripture, and the spiritual leadership of House of Worship Christian Church (HOWC). No revelation or spiritual office places any leader above responsible Christian conduct or ethical safeguarding requirements.
