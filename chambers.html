Ministry Inquiry

The Five Chambers of Ministry

Strategic ministry expressions unfolding progressively toward the planned 2027 horizon and beyond. Each chamber provides a structured vehicle for prayer, equipping, fellowship, digital media, and publishing.

01 — House of Prayer

The prayer expression of WCH, devoted to seeking God through intercession, worship and consecration. Expressions may include prophetic entreaties, prayer gatherings, intercession sessions, worship, fasting, consecration and revival meetings.

02 — School of Wisdom & Counsel (SWC)

The teaching expression of WCH. It equips people through Bible teaching, courses, seminars, workshops, ministry training, leadership development, wisdom teaching, prophetic instruction and practical life teaching. It is not presented as a formal theological institution.

03 — Company of Prophets (COP)

A relationship-based environment for people called to prophetic ministry or developing in the prophetic. Its purpose includes prophetic training and practice, accountability, study of the Scriptural prophets, fellowship and edification.

04 — Digital House

The media and digital expression of WCH, which may include livestreams, online teaching, audio teachings, podcasts, social media, digital courses, online prayer and digital resources.

05 — Publishing House

The publishing expression of WCH, devoted to the production of books, teaching materials, devotionals, study materials, and Christian literature for believers worldwide.

Meeting Locations

Wisdom and Counsel House (WCH) currently operates in the London Borough of Wandsworth, United Kingdom. Our community ministry gatherings are intended to take place in hired community halls, club rooms, and other suitable meeting spaces.

Our choice venues include Focus Hall and Newlands Hall, subject to availability and hire arrangements.

Hiring/Availability Venue Reference:
Focus Hall, Newlands Hall, Wandsworth, London SW18/SW19

Important notice: WCH does not currently have a permanent dedicated ministry building. All meeting locations are community/partner gatherings. Focus Hall and Newlands Hall are external hire venues, not permanent owned properties. Venues receive community rentals and leases, subject to booking and confirmation.
