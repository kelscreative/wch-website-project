Gatherings & Convocations

Wisdom and Counsel House convenes prayer meetings, seminars, theological workshops, School of Wisdom & Counsel training sessions, and Company of Prophets convocations. Each gathering announcement provides full event details, venue or online access links, schedule, and attendance coordination.

Status Labels in Use

To ensure complete truthfulness, WCH applies strict status labels across all proposed activities:

"Currently running": For verified ongoing activities.
"Confirmed": For confirmed convocations and events with settled details.
"Planned" or "In development": For initiatives that are not yet fully arranged.

Meeting Locations & Gathering Spaces

Wisdom and Counsel House (WCH) currently operates in the London Borough of Wandsworth, United Kingdom. Our community ministry gatherings are intended to take place in hired community halls, club rooms, and other suitable meeting spaces.

Our choice venues include Focus Hall and Newlands Hall, subject to availability and hire arrangements.

Venue Note: WCH does not currently have a permanent dedicated ministry building. All meeting locations are community/partner gatherings. Focus Hall and Newlands Hall are external hire venues, not permanent owned properties. Venues receive community rentals and leases, subject to booking and confirmation.
