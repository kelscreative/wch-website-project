Ministry Inquiry

Policies and Supporting Features

Wisdom and Counsel House is committed to total scriptural integrity, ethical transparency, legal compliance, and the highest standards of pastoral care.

Prophetic Ethics

Our public communications and ministry conduct reflect our foundational commitments:

  • Honour Scripture and Christ;
  • Respect human responsibility;
  • Avoid manipulation, intimidation, exploitation, unnecessary exposure of private information and dependency;
  • Welcome testing and accountability;
  • Recognise the possibility of human error;
  • Distinguish prophecy from personal opinion; and
  • Encourage obedience to God rather than to the prophet.

Safeguarding

Wisdom and Counsel House is dedicated to maintaining a safe, protected, and nurturing environment for all participants. Any safeguarding matters or inquiries may be brought directly to our attention via our official contact channels.

Ministry Disclaimer

Explain the scope of ministry content where appropriate. Biblical or pastoral counsel does not replace professional, medical, legal or other specialist services where required.

Privacy and Data Collection

Wisdom and Counsel House is committed to protecting your privacy. Personal information submitted through our official forms and channels is handled with confidentiality, used solely for communicating with you regarding your inquiry, and will never be shared without consent.

Operational & Ministry Standards

Prayer & Pastoral Inquiries: All prayer submissions and pastoral inquiries are handled with strict spiritual confidentiality, discretion, and biblical fidelity.
Communications & Updates: Ministry bulletins and teaching distributions are sent exclusively to individuals who have directly requested to receive communications.
Stewardship & Governance: Ministry operations adhere to rigorous integrity, transparency, and accountability standards in accordance with UK charity guidelines.
Gatherings & Registrations: Attendance for specialized convocations and training cohorts is managed through official coordination to ensure orderly and safe environments.
Intellectual Property & Media: All published monographs, audio discourses, and written materials are the intellectual property of Wisdom and Counsel House and Harrison Ihunwa Ministries.

Meeting Locations

Wisdom and Counsel House (WCH) currently operates in the London Borough of Wandsworth, United Kingdom. Our community ministry gatherings are intended to take place in hired community halls, club rooms, and other suitable meeting spaces.

Our choice venues include Focus Hall and Newlands Hall, subject to availability and hire arrangements.

Hiring/Availability Venue Reference:
Focus Hall, Newlands Hall, Wandsworth, London SW18/SW19

Important notice: WCH does not currently have a permanent dedicated ministry building. All meeting locations are community/partner gatherings. Focus Hall and Newlands Hall are external hire venues, not permanent owned properties. Venues receive community rentals and leases, subject to booking and confirmation.
